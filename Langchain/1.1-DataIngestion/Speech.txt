The Rise of Artificial Intelligence

Ladies and gentlemen,

We stand at a remarkable moment in human history. Artificial intelligence 
is no longer a concept from science fiction — it is here, it is real, 
and it is transforming every aspect of our lives.

From healthcare to education, from agriculture to finance, AI is solving 
problems that once seemed impossible. Doctors are using AI to detect cancer 
earlier than ever before. Farmers are using AI to predict weather patterns 
and improve crop yields. Students are using AI to learn at their own pace 
in their own language.

But with great power comes great responsibility. As we build these systems, 
we must ask ourselves — who benefits from this technology? Are we building 
AI that serves everyone, or only the privileged few?

The countries and companies that will lead tomorrow are not just those with 
the most powerful computers. They are those with the most thoughtful people — 
people who understand both the technical and human dimensions of this 
technology.

India stands at a unique position in this global AI revolution. With the 
largest young population in the world, with a tradition of mathematics and 
science, and with a growing startup ecosystem — India has everything it needs 
to become a world leader in artificial intelligence.

The question is not whether AI will change the world. It already is. 
The question is whether you will be a passenger watching it happen — 
or a builder shaping where it goes.

I urge each one of you — learn, build, and contribute. The future belongs 
to those who prepare for it today.

Thank you.
